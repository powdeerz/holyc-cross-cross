## About the Project
This project is an homage to the electrical engineer, computer programmer and outsider artist Terrence Andrew Davis. My goal is to modernize HolyC, while respecting his philosophy, simple and fast. I do not intend to gain money or fame out of this. This is a study in Computer Science that wishes to honor his legacy.
Terry's trajectory is a painful reminder of the importance of mental health awareness and support, especially severe disorders such as schizophrenia.
